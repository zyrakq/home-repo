# Graver work

- The `graver` plugin is global (`.opencode/global-plugins/graver`).
- When working with graver, the target projects are the stacks under
  `collection/docker-stack-collection`.
- Before preparing deploy configs with graver, read
  `collection/docker-stack-collection/AGENTS.md` and follow its instructions
  (infra roles, secrets solution, rules).
